AITP

Latest Release: Dec 2025

Precision medicine tool for rheumatoid arthritis (RA) to recommend best drug treatment.

RA is a heterogenous autoimmune disease. Drugs work on some patients and fail on others. RA patients have different types of dysregulated immune cells, known as disease heterogeneity. Medicines for RA target specific causes of inflammation (Tumor Necrosis Factor-alpha, Interleukin 6, B cells) caused by specific dysregulated immune cells. Dysregulated immune cell type is not easily determined from traditional diagnosis, but synovium biopsy scRNAseq data of a patient processed with AITP machine learning model determines dysregulated immune cell types and numbers (abundance). AITP uses these immune cell types and numbers with known biologic drug mechanisms in a transparent and explainable rule-based approach to determine best drug treatment.

Based on research by Soumya Raychaudhuri at Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard, Boston, United States.

HUMANGEO

Release: Q1 2026

Metabolism digital twin precisely pinpoints metabolic dysregulation and gives machines an understanding of human metabolism by composing medical reports like kidney function test (KFT), liver function test (LFT), complete blood count (CBC) and urine routine in a single vector.

Humangeo can be used by human doctors to diagnose early metabolic diseases like nonalcoholic fatty liver and type 2 diabetes by pinpointing exact dysregulated pathways in patient metabolism. Patient data from medical reports are processed by Humangeo for real-time patient metabolism. Systemic and organ-specific metabolic reactions with degree of dysregulation are highlighted.

Developed by ONITO's founder Noorul Ali at Tufts University, Boston, United States.
